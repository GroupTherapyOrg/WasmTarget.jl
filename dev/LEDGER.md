# Dale's requirements — the ledger

Every requirement Dale has stated for WasmTarget, in his words, with why he wants it and the checks
that enforce it. `dev/CHARTER.md` cites this file; `test/parity_ratchet.jl` reads it (L204, L205,
L206). A requirement lives here the day Dale states it, never only in an agent's memory or a note.

How the table is kept:

- IDs only increase and are never reused or renumbered. A requirement Dale replaces or drops is deleted
  with its row, never kept marked superseded (Dale 2026-11-04, D49): a stale rule is bloat every agent reads.
- `enforced by` names at least one check: a lock or ratchet of `test/parity_ratchet.jl` (`L<n>`,
  `R<n>`), a charter clause (`C<n>`, meaning every check that clause cites), or a test, hook, script
  or workflow by its path. A requirement no check enforces yet names its MARCH row,
  `planned: <row>`, where its planned check is written; L204 fails on a row with neither.
- Every check in `test/parity_ratchet.jl` serves a row, directly or through a clause a row cites
  (L204): a check that serves no requirement is noise.
- A row that states a number carries its intent and its band: the number is a target, and the check
  fails only outside the band. A check firing inside its band is a bug in the check (L205).
- The `rule for agents` cell of every row is generated into AGENTS.md, in order (L206), so
  every agent reads every requirement.
